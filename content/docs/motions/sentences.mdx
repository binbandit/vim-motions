These are **cursor motions**. For operating on the sentence/paragraph *under* the
cursor, prefer text objects <Keys keys="is" link /> / <Keys keys="as" link /> /
<Keys keys="ip" link /> / <Keys keys="ap" link />.

## Sentence motions

<MotionList>
  <Motion
    keys="("
    name="Sentence(s) backward"
    before={'One. Two. |Three.'}
    after={'One. |Two. Three.'}
  />
  <Motion
    keys=")"
    name="Sentence(s) forward"
    before={'One. |Two. Three.'}
    after={'One. Two. |Three.'}
  />
</MotionList>

A **sentence** ends at `.` `!` or `?` followed by EOL, space, or tab.

## Paragraph motions

`{` / `}` land on the **blank line** that separates paragraphs (not on the
paragraph text itself).

<MotionList>
  <Motion
    keys="{"
    name="Paragraph(s) backward"
    before={'para one\n\npara |two\n\npara three'}
    after={'para one\n|\npara two\n\npara three'}
    note="Jumps to the empty line above the current paragraph."
  />
  <Motion
    keys="}"
    name="Paragraph(s) forward"
    before={'para |one\n\npara two\n\npara three'}
    after={'para one\n|\npara two\n\npara three'}
    note="Jumps to the empty line below the current paragraph."
  />
</MotionList>

A **paragraph** begins after each **empty** line (not merely whitespace-only).

## With operators

<Buffer
  keys="d)"
  before={'Keep. |Delete this sentence. Stay.'}
  after={'Keep. |Stay.'}
/>

<Buffer
  keys="y}"
  before={'|# include header\n\nbody text'}
  after={'|# include header\n\nbody text'}
  note="Yanks from the cursor through the next paragraph boundary into the default register."
/>

<Buffer
  keys="dap"
  before={'keep\n\n|delete this paragraph\nstill here\n\nkeep'}
  after={'keep\n\n|keep'}
  note="Usually better than d} when you want the whole paragraph including surrounding blank lines."
/>
