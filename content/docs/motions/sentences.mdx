These are **text object motions** (they move the cursor). For operating on a sentence/paragraph under the cursor, prefer text objects <Keys keys="is" /> / <Keys keys="as" /> / <Keys keys="ip" /> / <Keys keys="ap" />.

## Sentence motions

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="(" name="Sentence(s) backward" example="[count]( — exclusive" />
  <Motion keys=")" name="Sentence(s) forward" />
</MotionList>

A **sentence** ends at `.` `!` or `?` followed by EOL, space, or tab. Closing `)` `]` `"` `'` may appear after the punctuation. Paragraph and section boundaries also count as sentence boundaries.

## Paragraph motions

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="{" name="Paragraph(s) backward" />
  <Motion keys="}" name="Paragraph(s) forward" />
</MotionList>

A **paragraph** begins after each **empty** line (not merely a blank whitespace-only line), and at nroff-style macros from the `'paragraphs'` option. Section boundaries are also paragraph boundaries.

## With operators

| Combo | Result |
| --- | --- |
| <Keys keys="d)" /> | Delete to next sentence |
| <Keys keys="c(" /> | Change back through previous sentence |
| <Keys keys="y}" /> | Yank to next paragraph |
| <Keys keys=">}" /> | Indent through next paragraph |
| <Keys keys="dap" /> | Delete **a** paragraph (usually better than `d}`) |
| <Keys keys="vip" /> | Visually select inner paragraph |

## Related mark motions

| Keys | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `` `( `` / <Keys keys="'(" /> | Start of current sentence |
| `` `) `` / <Keys keys="')" /> | End of current sentence |
| `` `{ `` / <Keys keys="'{" /> | Start of current paragraph |
| `` `} `` / <Keys keys="'}" /> | End of current paragraph |

See [Marks as motions](/docs/motions/marks-as-motions).
