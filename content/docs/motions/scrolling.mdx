Scrolling moves the **window**; some commands also move the cursor.

## Page & half-page

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-f" name="Page forward" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-b" name="Page backward" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-d" name="Half-page down" example="Also moves cursor down" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-u" name="Half-page up" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-e" name="Scroll down one line" example="Cursor stays if possible" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-y" name="Scroll up one line" />
</MotionList>

## Redraw cursor position

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="zz" name="Center cursor line" />
  <Motion keys="zt" name="Cursor line to top" />
  <Motion keys="zb" name="Cursor line to bottom" />
  <Motion keys="zs" name="Scroll horizontally: cursor to left" />
  <Motion keys="ze" name="Scroll horizontally: cursor to right" />
  <Motion keys="zh zl" name="Scroll left / right one character" />
  <Motion keys="zH zL" name="Scroll half screen left / right" />
</MotionList>

## Window-relative moves

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="H" name="Top of window (Home)" />
  <Motion keys="M" name="Middle of window" />
  <Motion keys="L" name="Bottom of window (Low)" />
</MotionList>

With counts: <Keys keys="3H" /> → third line from top of window.

## Relative line numbers tip

Many configs use `relativenumber` so you can type <Keys keys="12j" /> instead of searching. Pair with <Keys keys="zz" /> after big jumps to stay oriented.
