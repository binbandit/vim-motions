## Window-relative motions

These **are** motions (usable with operators: `yH`, `dL`, …):

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="H" name="To [count]th line from top of window" example="Default first visible · linewise · Home" />
  <Motion keys="M" name="Middle line of window" example="Linewise" />
  <Motion keys="L" name="To [count]th line from bottom of window" example="Default last visible · linewise · Low" />
</MotionList>

Cursor lands on the first non-blank (see `'startofline'`). Adjusted for `'scrolloff'` unless an operator is pending.

## Page & half-page (move cursor + view)

From `:help scroll.txt` — these move the cursor as they scroll:

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-f" name="Page forward" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-b" name="Page backward" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-d" name="Half-page down" example="Also moves cursor down" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-u" name="Half-page up" />
</MotionList>

## View-only scroll (not operator motions)

Cursor tries to stay put:

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-e" name="Scroll down one line" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-y" name="Scroll up one line" />
  <Motion keys="zh" name="Scroll left one character" />
  <Motion keys="zl" name="Scroll right one character" />
  <Motion keys="zH" name="Scroll half screen left" />
  <Motion keys="zL" name="Scroll half screen right" />
  <Motion keys="zs" name="Scroll: cursor to leftmost screen column" />
  <Motion keys="ze" name="Scroll: cursor to rightmost screen column" />
</MotionList>

## Redraw cursor line

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="zz" name="Center cursor line" />
  <Motion keys="zt" name="Cursor line to top of window" />
  <Motion keys="zb" name="Cursor line to bottom of window" />
  <Motion keys="z." name="Like zz then go to first non-blank" />
  <Motion keys="z-" name="Like zb then go to first non-blank" />
  <Motion keys="zEnter" name="Like zt then go to first non-blank" example="z&lt;CR&gt;" />
</MotionList>

## Tip

With `'relativenumber'`, type <Keys keys="12j" /> instead of searching. Pair big jumps with <Keys keys="zz" /> to stay oriented.
