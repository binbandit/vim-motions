Vim has two notions of “word” (`:help word-motions`):

- **word** (`w`/`e`/`b`/`ge`) — letters, digits, underscores as one class; punctuation is separate
- **WORD** (`W`/`E`/`B`/`gE`) — non-blank sequences separated only by whitespace

Cursor shown as the highlighted cell (`|` in the source).

## Forward / back

<MotionList>
  <Motion
    keys="w"
    name="Next word start"
    also={['Shift-→']}
    before={'const |userName = 1'}
    after={'const userName |= 1'}
    note="From u, w jumps to = (punctuation is its own word)."
  />
  <Motion
    keys="W"
    name="Next WORD start"
    also={['Ctrl-→']}
    before={'foo|.bar baz'}
    after={'foo.bar |baz'}
    note="W treats foo.bar as one WORD — skips the dot."
  />
  <Motion
    keys="e"
    name="End of word"
    before={'const |userName = 1'}
    after={'const userNam|e = 1'}
    note="Inclusive — cursor lands on the last character of the word."
  />
  <Motion
    keys="E"
    name="End of WORD"
    before={'|foo.bar baz'}
    after={'foo.ba|r baz'}
  />
  <Motion
    keys="b"
    name="Previous word start"
    also={['Shift-←']}
    before={'const userName| = 1'}
    after={'const |userName = 1'}
  />
  <Motion
    keys="B"
    name="Previous WORD start"
    also={['Ctrl-←']}
    before={'foo.bar |baz'}
    after={'|foo.bar baz'}
  />
  <Motion
    keys="ge"
    name="End of previous word"
    before={'const userName |= 1'}
    after={'const userNam|e = 1'}
    note="From =, ge lands on e of userName."
  />
  <Motion
    keys="gE"
    name="End of previous WORD"
    before={'foo.bar |baz'}
    after={'foo.ba|r baz'}
  />
</MotionList>

## With operators

<Buffer
  keys="dw"
  before={'delete |word here'}
  after={'delete |here'}
  note="Deletes from the cursor to the next word start — includes the trailing space."
/>

<Buffer
  keys="de"
  before={'delete |word here'}
  after={'delete | here'}
  note="Deletes through the end of the word — keeps the following space."
/>

<Buffer
  keys="cw"
  before={'delete |word here'}
  after={'delete |X here'}
  note="Special: cw acts like ce inside a word — keeps the trailing space (unlike dw)."
/>

<Buffer
  keys="diw"
  before={'edit the |userName| now'}
  after={'edit the | now'}
  note="Text object — deletes the whole word under the cursor regardless of where in the word you are."
/>

<Buffer
  keys="daw"
  before={'edit the |userName| now'}
  after={'edit the |now'}
  note="Deletes the word and a neighboring space."
/>

## Operator tips

| Combo | Why |
| --- | --- |
| <Keys keys="dw" /> | To next word start (includes trailing space) |
| <Keys keys="de" /> | Through end of word (keeps following space) |
| <Keys keys="cw" /> | Like <Keys keys="ce" /> — keeps trailing space |
| <Keys keys="diw" link /> | Whole word under cursor |
| <Keys keys="daw" link /> | Word **and** surrounding whitespace |
