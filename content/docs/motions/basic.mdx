These are the smallest cursor steps. Master them before anything else.

## Directional

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="h" name="Left" example="One character left (same as ←)" />
  <Motion keys="j" name="Down" example="One line down (same as ↓)" />
  <Motion keys="k" name="Up" example="One line up (same as ↑)" />
  <Motion keys="l" name="Right" example="One character right (same as →)" />
</MotionList>

Prefer <Keys keys="h j k l" /> over arrow keys — your hands stay on home row.

## Character / screen cell

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Space" name="Right (like l)" />
  <Motion keys="Backspace" name="Left (like h), can cross lines" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-h" name="Same as Backspace in Normal" />
</MotionList>

## Useful habits

| Goal | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| Next word | <Keys keys="w" /> not many <Keys keys="l" /> |
| End of line | <Keys keys="$" /> not holding <Keys keys="l" /> |
| Matching paren | <Keys keys="%" /> |
| Find on line | <Keys keys="f" /> / <Keys keys="t" /> |

## With operators

<Keys keys="dl" /> deletes the character under the cursor (same idea as <Keys keys="x" />).
<Keys keys="dh" /> deletes the character to the left (same idea as <Keys keys="X" />).
<Keys keys="dj" /> deletes this line and the next.
