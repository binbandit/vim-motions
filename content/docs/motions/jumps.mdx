Far motions push the previous position onto the **jump list**. Near motions (like `h`/`w`) usually do not.

## Jump list

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-o" name="Older jump" example="Back through jump history" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-i" name="Newer jump" example="Forward · Tab often sends this" />
  <Motion keys=":jumps" name="Show jump list" />
  <Motion keys=":clearjumps" name="Clear jump list" />
  <Motion keys="''" name="Line before the latest jump" />
  <Motion keys="``" name="Exact position before the latest jump" />
</MotionList>

Use <Keys keys="g'{mark}" /> / <Keys keys="g`{mark}" /> to jump to a mark **without** adding a jumplist entry.

## Change list

Positions of edits, separate from jumps:

</MotionList>

## Motions that typically jump

Examples that usually add jumplist entries (not exhaustive):

- <Keys keys="'" /> / <Keys keys="`" /> marks
- <Keys keys="G" /> <Keys keys="gg" /> <Keys keys="{count}%" /> <Keys keys="/" /> <Keys keys="?" /> <Keys keys="n" /> <Keys keys="N" />
- <Keys keys="(" /> <Keys keys=")" /> <Keys keys="{" /> <Keys keys="}" />
- <Keys keys="[[" /> <Keys keys="]]" /> <Keys keys="H" /> <Keys keys="L" /> <Keys keys="M" /> (often)
- <Keys keys="%" /> when it moves far
- <Keys keys="*" /> <Keys keys="#" /> <Keys keys="gd" /> <Keys keys="gD" />

Exact rules: `:help jump-motions`.
