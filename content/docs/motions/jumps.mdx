## Matching pairs

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="%" name="Jump to matching pair" example="On (, ), [, ], {, }, /* */, #if/#endif, and more" />
</MotionList>

With a count, <Keys keys="%" /> jumps to that percentage of the file (`50%` → midpoint).

## Section / method style jumps

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="[[" name="Previous `{` in column 1" />
  <Motion keys="]]" name="Next `{` in column 1" />
  <Motion keys="[]" name="Previous `}` in column 1" />
  <Motion keys="][" name="Next `}` in column 1" />
  <Motion keys="[{" name="Previous unmatched `{`" />
  <Motion keys="]}" name="Next unmatched `{`" />
  <Motion keys="[(" name="Previous unmatched `(`" />
  <Motion keys="])" name="Next unmatched `(`" />
</MotionList>

Exact behavior depends on `filetype` and definitions of sections — great in C-like code; languages may remap.

## Jump list

Motions that move “far” push to the jump list.

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-o" name="Older jump" example="Back through jump history" />
  <Motion keys="Ctrl-i" name="Newer jump" example="Forward (Tab often sends this)" />
  <Motion keys=":jumps" name="Show jump list" />
  <Motion keys="''" name="Previous jump / last position line" example="Back to last line before a jump" />
  <Motion keys="``" name="Exact previous position" />
  <Motion keys="g;" name="Older change position" />
  <Motion keys="g," name="Newer change position" />
  <Motion keys=":changes" name="Show change list" />
</MotionList>
