---
title: Counts
description: Prefix numbers to multiply motions and operators.
---

Type a number before a command to repeat it.

<Motion
  keys="3w"
  name="Move 3 words forward"
  before={"|one two three four five"}
  after={"one two three |four five"}
/>

<Motion
  keys="2dw"
  name="Delete 2 words"
  before={"|one two three four"}
  after={"|three four"}
  note="Count on the operator — equivalent to d2w."
/>

<Motion
  keys="3dd"
  name="Delete 3 lines"
  before={"keep\n|a\nb\nc\nkeep"}
  after={"keep\n|keep"}
/>

<Motion
  keys="2fx"
  name="Find the 2nd x on the line"
  before={"|axbxcx"}
  after={"axb|xcx"}
/>

<MotionList>
  <Motion keys="5j" name="Move down 5 lines" />
  <Motion keys="10." name="Repeat last change 10 times" />
  <Motion keys="3p" name="Paste 3 times" />
</MotionList>

## Count placement

Both of these work:

- <Keys keys="2dw" link /> — count on the operator
- <Keys keys="d2w" link /> — count on the motion

They usually mean the same thing. Prefer whichever you type more fluently.

## Counts with Insert

<Keys keys="5i*-Esc" /> inserts `*****` (five stars). Counts before <Keys keys="i" /> / <Keys keys="a" /> / <Keys keys="I" /> / <Keys keys="A" /> / <Keys keys="o" /> / <Keys keys="O" /> repeat the inserted text after you leave Insert.

## Line numbers

<Keys keys="42G" /> or <Keys keys="42gg" /> jumps to line 42. <Keys keys=":" />`42` <Keys keys="Enter" /> does the same.
